title: Privacy
description: What MyGist stores, why it stores it, and how to get rid of it.
---

Short version. If you join the waitlist, MyGist stores your email address and
nothing else. If you have an account, your persona lives in a Postgres database
as readable JSON, and you can export or delete all of it whenever you like.

## The waitlist

Leaving your address on the front page stores one row, holding your email
address, the date you joined, and the date an invite was sent if one has been.
The address is trimmed and lower-cased first, so the same address cannot take
two slots.

It is used for one thing, which is sending you an invite when a slot opens.
There is no mailing list and no newsletter attached to it.

Joining twice is not an error and does not store anything twice. The server
also answers the same way whether or not an address is already on the list,
deliberately, so that the form cannot be used to find out whether somebody
you know has signed up.

To be taken off the list before an invite arrives, email
[liam@thuradev.qzz.io](mailto:liam@thuradev.qzz.io) and the row is deleted.

## Your persona

Once you have an account, everything you put in MyGist lives in a Postgres
database as JSON, one row per section. You can read all of it, export all of
it, and delete all of it — **Account → Data → Export**, or ask your assistant
for `get_raw`. [FAQ](/use/faq) covers this in more detail.

Every read and write is scoped to the account behind the credential, so other
people using the same server cannot see your persona.

If you are running your own instance, that database is yours and this page
describes somebody else's server. If somebody else hosts your instance, the
database is theirs, and you should trust them accordingly.

## What leaves the server

Two cases, both covered on [Capture](/use/capture) and in the
[FAQ](/use/faq).

**To your AI client, when it asks.** That is the entire point of MyGist, and it
means your persona reaches whatever provider is behind the client you connected.
Nothing is sent unless a client calls a tool — there is no background process
reading your conversations.

**To an embedding provider, if the instance is configured with one.** Text is
sent away to compute search vectors. A self-hosted instance can use a local
embedding server, or none at all. [Database](/run/database) explains how.

## What there is not

No analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts on the front page
or in the app. No advertising. Nothing is sold or shared.

## Changes

MyGist is invite-only and small, and this page will change as it grows. It
describes how the thing behaves today rather than reserving rights for later.
