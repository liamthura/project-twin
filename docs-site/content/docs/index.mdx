Every new AI conversation starts from nothing. You re-explain your role, your
stack, how you like answers written, what you are working on. MyGist stores that
once and serves it to any client that speaks
[MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io), so the context travels with you instead
of living in one vendor's memory.

Your persona is structured JSON in your own Postgres — readable, exportable, and
editable by hand or through the web UI. Nothing is inferred behind your back:
tools only run when a client calls them.

## The shape of it

A persona is divided into **sections** — profile, goals, knowledge, preferences,
projects, lifestyle, circle and learning log, plus opt-in media and aesthetics.
Each section is one JSONB blob per user, defined by a declarative *pack* that
also states what an AI client may write into it.

Reads are **scoped**, so a client asks for `minimal` or `professional` rather
than pulling everything. Writes go through a small vocabulary of entities that
`get_schema` publishes — that is the authoritative list, and these docs teach
you to ask it rather than keeping a copy that can drift.
