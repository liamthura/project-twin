Every new AI conversation starts from nothing. You explain your role again. Your
stack again. How you like answers written, and what you are in the middle of.

MyGist stores all of that once and hands it to any AI assistant that asks. Your
context follows you between tools instead of being locked inside one of them.

## What it is, in one screen

**Your data lives in your own Postgres database.** It is plain, readable JSON.
You can view it, edit it, export it, and delete it. There is no proprietary
format to get stuck in.

**Assistants connect over [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io)** — the Model
Context Protocol, an open standard for giving AI clients access to tools and
data. Claude Desktop, Claude Code and a growing list of others speak it.

**Nothing happens unless a client asks.** MyGist does not watch your
conversations. It answers questions and it accepts changes, and it does neither
on its own. [Capture](/use/capture) explains what that means day to day.

**Assistants read a slice, not the lot.** A client asks for a *scope* — say
`professional` — and gets a summary sized for it. When it needs something
specific, it searches instead of loading more. That is how a persona can grow
for years without every conversation paying for it.

## What you can store

Ten sections, covering the things that usually get re-explained:

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Name, role, where you work, education, languages, contact |
| **Goals** | What you are working toward, and how it is going |
| **Knowledge** | Subjects you know, and topics you are currently tracking |
| **Preferences** | How you like code written, and answers written |
| **Projects** | What you are building, and what is top of mind this week |
| **Lifestyle** | Hobbies, values, personality, routines |
| **Circle** | People in your life and how you relate to them |
| **Learning log** | Things you learned, decided, or realised |
| **Media** *(optional)* | Films, books, music, games — taste, not a watchlist |
| **Aesthetics** *(optional)* | Visual styles you like and dislike |

Each one is covered on [What goes in each section](/use/sections).

Talk to your assistant, or open the web editor. Both write to the same place,
and neither is the "real" one.
