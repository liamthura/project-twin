Every new AI conversation starts from nothing. You explain your role again, your
stack again, how you like answers written, and what you are in the middle of.

MyGist stores all of that once and hands it to any AI assistant that asks, so
your context follows you between tools.

## What it is

Your data lives in your own Postgres database as plain, readable JSON. You can
view it, edit it, export it, and delete it.

Assistants connect over [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io), the Model
Context Protocol. It is an open standard for giving AI clients access to tools
and data, and Claude Desktop, Claude Code and a growing list of others speak it.

Nothing happens unless a client asks. MyGist does not watch your conversations;
it answers questions and accepts changes, and only when a client calls a tool.
[Capture](/use/capture) explains what that means day to day.

Assistants read a slice. A client asks for a *scope*, say `professional`, and
gets a summary sized for it; when it needs something specific it searches. That
is how a persona can grow for years without every conversation paying for it.

## What you can store

Ten sections, covering the things that usually get re-explained:

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Name, role, where you work, education, languages, contact |
| **Goals** | What you are working toward, and how it is going |
| **Knowledge** | Subjects you know, and topics you are currently tracking |
| **Preferences** | How you like code written, and answers written |
| **Projects** | What you are building, and what is top of mind this week |
| **Lifestyle** | Hobbies, values, personality, routines |
| **Circle** | People in your life and how you relate to them |
| **Learning log** | Things you learned, decided, or realised |
| **Media** *(optional)* | Films, books, music, games: taste, not a watchlist |
| **Aesthetics** *(optional)* | Visual styles you like and dislike |

Each one is covered on [What goes in each section](/use/sections).

## Editing your persona

Talk to your assistant, or open the web editor. Both write to the same place, so
it makes no difference which you use.
