The web UI at `/app/` on your instance is a full editor for everything MyGist
stores. Open it in a browser and sign in with your username and password. The
root of the instance is the landing page, with a link to the app.

It is often the faster tool. Correcting six fields is a few clicks, against
several turns of conversation.

## Getting around

Sections appear in the rail down the left. Within a section:

- **Lists** show one row per entry. Click a row to expand it and see its fields.
- **Scalar fields** such as your name, role and bio are plain forms.
- **Tag-style fields** are chips: type to add, click the × to remove.
- **Nested lists** live inside an expanded row. A job's highlights and skills, a
  course's topics, a project's references.

## Saving

Auto-save is on by default. Changes are written a moment after you stop typing,
and the status next to the toggle tells you where things stand: *Saving…*,
*Saved just now*, or *Unsaved changes*.

Turn auto-save off and a **Save now** button appears instead, so you can make a
batch of edits and commit them in one go. The status then reads *Unsaved
changes* until you press it.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/editor-entry-expanded.png"
  caption="An expanded list entry"
/>

The editor is **generated from the same section packs** the server writes
through. A pack that declares a field gets a control for it. That is why the UI
and the AI tool vocabulary stay in step: there is no second definition to fall
out of date.

## On a phone

The editor works on a small screen. A few things move:

- Entry titles get the full width of the row, and status chips drop to a second
  line underneath.
- Section navigation collapses from the rail into a dropdown.

## Sections

Sections are switched on and off in **Settings → Sections**, which **Manage
sections** at the bottom of the menu also opens.

- **Media**, **aesthetics** and **inventory** start off. Switch them on if you
  want them.
- **Profile**, **preferences** and the **learning log** are always on.
- Everything else is on by default and can be switched off.

A section that is off leaves the editor, and AI clients can no longer read or
change it. Nothing in it is deleted: switch it back on and it returns exactly
as it was.

## History

Every section header has a **History** button. It lists the last versions of
that section, kept automatically whenever anything changes it, with the date
and which client made the change. **Restore this** puts a version back, and a
restore can itself be undone the same way.

## Where an entry came from

Open an entry and the line under its fields says who added it and who last
changed it, and when: you, an app writing directly, or an assistant's
suggestion you approved in Review. For one that came through Review, **Why?**
shows the assistant's reasoning and the words of yours it quoted. Entries from
before MyGist kept this record show only when they last changed.

Goals and Projects go **Stale** after four months without a change, and
Knowledge after a year. A stale entry is marked on its row, and the list's
header shows how many there are: click the count to see only those. Open one
to edit or remove it, or choose **Keep** if it is still right. Keep restarts
the clock without changing the entry, and assistants stop seeing it as stale
too.

## Account

Open **Settings** from the menu under your name, top right. The same menu
switches the theme and signs you out. Settings is a page of its own, with four
tabs: **Account**, **Connections**, **Sections** (above) and **Data**.

### Account

Your password: set one if you registered without, or change the one you have.
Minimum eight characters. Your email, linked sign-in accounts, and whether
changes save as you type are here too.

### Connections

Everything that can reach your persona, in two groups: apps connected over
OAuth, and tokens you issued yourself. Issue named tokens for AI clients, see
when each was last used, and revoke any of them.

A new token can read your persona and suggest changes for your approval.
Changing it directly is off unless you switch it on, because those changes skip
your review queue.

<Callout type="warn">
A new token's value is shown **once**, when you create it. Copy it immediately.
It cannot be read back, only replaced.
</Callout>

Use one token per client, so revoking a token from a machine you no longer have
does not disconnect everything else.

### Data

**Export** downloads your whole persona as a zip of JSON files, one per section:
the same structured data the tools read.

**Import** takes one back. Choose the mode first, then the file:

| Mode | Does |
|---|---|
| **Merge** | Adds the backup to what is already there. The default |
| **Replace** | Overwrites your existing data with the backup's contents, after asking once more |

Export then import is also how you move between servers. It needs no database
access at either end.

**Advanced: server**, at the bottom of Data, is which instance this web app
talks to. You only need it when the page is not being served by the instance
itself.

## Editor-only actions

- **Bulk corrections** that would be tedious one tool call at a time.
- **Seeing everything at once**, including fields no read scope surfaces.
- **Fixing a bad write**: if a client stored something under the wrong entry,
  correcting it here is immediate.
- **Exporting and importing** your data.

## Tool-only actions

- **Linking entries.** The editor shows relationships but does not author them.
  See [Relations](/use/relations).
- **Capture suggestions**, which are a conversation feature by nature.

## Editing in both at once

<Callout>
Both write to the same rows. There is no sync step and no conflict resolution:
whichever wrote last wins.

So avoid editing the same entry in the browser and in a conversation at the same
moment. Between the two, at different times, is completely fine.
</Callout>
