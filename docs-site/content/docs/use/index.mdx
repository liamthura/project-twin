Once a client is connected, MyGist gives it three things: a **scoped read** of
who you are, a **write path** for keeping that current, and a **search** over
everything you have stored.

## What a client sees

At the start of a conversation a well-behaved client calls `get_context` and
receives a compact summary — name, role, how you like answers written, what you
are working on. Not your whole persona: a scope.

When something specific comes up it searches rather than loading more:

```text
search_context("postgres")  →  ~10 ranked snippets with entity ids
get_entity(["proj_a1b2"])   →  full detail for just the ones that matter
```

That two-step is the pattern the tools are designed around, and it is why a
persona can grow without every conversation paying for it.

## What it cannot do

MyGist tools are **passive**. They run when a client calls them and never
otherwise. Nothing watches your conversations, and nothing is written without an
explicit call — see [Capture](/use/capture) for what that means in practice
and why it is a deliberate limitation rather than a missing feature.
